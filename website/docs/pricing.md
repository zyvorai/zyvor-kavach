# Pricing

Kavach is free for evaluation and non-production use, with a 30-day evaluation. A production licence is per endpoint per year: INR including 18% GST in India, US dollars excluding tax elsewhere.

| Plan | India (INR incl. 18% GST) | Worldwide (USD excl. tax) | Minimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kavach Pro | INR 3,999 per endpoint per year | USD 40 per endpoint per year | 25 endpoints (INR 99,975 / USD 1,009) |
| Kavach Enterprise | INR 6,499 per endpoint per year | USD 66 per endpoint per year | 100 endpoints (INR 649,900 / USD 6,557) |

- A server counts as 3 endpoints.
- Volume: 256-499 endpoints -10%, 500-999 -15%, 1,000 or more -20%. Multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%. Volume and multi-year together are capped at 25%.
- Kavach observes and reports only; it never contains, kills or isolates.
- After payment you receive a GST invoice (India) or a US-dollar invoice, and the Zyvor team issues your licence and helps with onboarding. Nothing renews automatically.
