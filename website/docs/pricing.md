# Pricing

Kavach is free for evaluation and non-production use, with a 30-day evaluation. A production licence is per endpoint per year, in rupees including 18% GST.

| Plan | Price | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Kavach Pro | INR 3,999 per endpoint per year (about USD 40) | 25 endpoints (INR 99,975) |
| Kavach Enterprise | INR 6,499 per endpoint per year (about USD 66) | 100 endpoints (INR 649,900) |

- A server counts as 3 endpoints.
- Volume: 500 or more endpoints -15%, 1,000 or more -20%. Multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%.
- Kavach observes and reports only; it never contains, kills or isolates.
- After payment you receive a GST invoice, and the Zyvor team issues your licence and helps with onboarding. Nothing renews automatically.

## How to pay

- Online: [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy#kavach) (UPI, cards and netbanking through Razorpay; opens shortly).
- Invoice or quote (teams, volume, multi-year, USD): [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev).

The rupee price is the real price. USD figures are for reference at INR / 1.18 / 84. The licence terms are in the [Zyvor Production License v1.0](https://github.com/zyvorai/zyvor-kavach/blob/main/LICENSE).
